About FORMATIX

A Creative Production Partner.

FORMATIX brings design thinking and practical production together to help businesses create stronger physical brand experiences. We focus on the connection between the idea, the space, the materials and the final result.

Whether the brief involves an exhibition stand, a corporate event, branded displays or advertising production, our approach starts with understanding your objective and planning the work around it.

  • Creative concepts and 3D visualization
  • Practical planning and production coordination
  • Attention to materials, finishing and presentation
  • Solutions tailored to the project brief
